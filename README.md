# Sales & Profit Dashboard

## 1. Project Title
Sales & Profit Dashboard

## 2. Purpose
A business intelligence dashboard designed to track **sales performance, profit trends, and customer growth** across multiple years, product categories, and regions.  
The purpose is to provide actionable insights for decision-making in sales strategy and resource allocation.

## 3. Tech Stack (Key Technologies Used)
- Power BI (for visualization)
- SQL (for querying and preparing data)
- Python (for preprocessing)
- GitHub (for version control and documentation)

## 4. Data Source
- Sample Superstore Dataset (commonly used in BI projects)  
- Or custom sales transaction records (Order Date, Sales, Profit, Customer, Category, State)

## 5. Features / Highlights
- **Business Problem:** Lack of visibility into sales and profit distribution across products, customers, and regions.  
- **Goal of Dashboard:** Provide a **single view** of KPIs (Sales, Profit, Customer Count) to identify growth opportunities and weak areas.  
- **Key Visuals:**
  - Total Profit & Sales cards (quick KPIs)
  - Yearly Profit Trend (bar chart)
  - Sales by Sub-Category (horizontal bar chart)
  - Customer Count by Year (donut chart)
  - Sales by State (regional performance)
- **Business Impacts & Insights:**
  - Identify top-performing categories (e.g., Chairs, Machines, Phones)
  - Spot declining profit trends in 2024
  - Recognize customer growth year-on-year
  - Highlight regional sales concentration (California leading)
