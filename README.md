# Sales-Dashboard
Project Title : Sales & Profit Dashboard
Purpose:
A business intelligence dashboard designed to track sales performance, profit trends, and customer growth across multiple years, product categories, and regions. The purpose is to provide actionable insights for decision-making in sales strategy and resource allocation.

Teck Stack(key technologies used to build the dashboard
-Power BI / Tableau / Excel (depending on what you used for visualization)
-SQL (for querying and preparing data)
-Python / Pandas (if preprocessing was done programmatically)
-GitHub (for version control and documentation)

Data Source
   Sample Superstore Dataset (commonly used in BI projects)
   
Features/Highlights:
   
Business Problem: Lack of visibility into sales and profit distribution across products, customers, and regions.

Goal of Dashboard: Provide a single view of KPIs (Sales, Profit, Customer Count) to identify growth opportunities and weak areas.

Key Visuals:
1.Total Profit & Sales cards (quick KPIs)
2.Yearly Profit Trend (bar chart)
3.Sales by Sub-Category (horizontal bar chart)
4.Customer Count by Year (donut chart)
5.Sales by State (regional performance)

Business Impacts & Insights:
1.Identify top-performing categories (e.g., Chairs, Machines, Phones)
2.Spot declining profit trends in 2024
3.Recognize customer growth year-on-year
4.Highlight regional sales concentration (California leading)
